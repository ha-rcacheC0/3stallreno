Antiques

Antiques are generally defined as items that are at least 100 years old, this gives each item its own unique history and character. Often antiques are valued for their age, craftsmanship, history, and uniqueness. Here at Third Stall Renovation we carefully seek out interesting antiques that are enjoyable to own, display, and appreciate as a great addition to a home or collection.

Collectibles

Unlike traditional antiques, which generally must be at least 100 years old, collectibles can be brand-new, mass-produced items that people believe will grow in value due to an item's rarity, condition and demand. Consumer interest, cultural nostalgia, or pop-culture popularity determines how sought-after an item is at any given time.

Vintage Decor

Vintage decor refers to interior design items, furniture, and accessories that are typically 20 to 99 years old. Common materials of vintage decor include aged wood, cast iron, brass, distressed leather, heavy glass, stone, and natural linens. Vintage covers distinct eras like Mid-Century Modern, Art Deco, Victorian, and 1930s Cottage.
