Antiques

Antiques are generally defined as items that are at least 100 years old, this gives each item its own unique history and character. Often antiques are valued for their age, craftsmanship, history, and uniqueness. Here at Third Stall Renovation we carefully seek out interesting antiques that are enjoyable to own, display, and appreciate as a great addition to a home or collection.
